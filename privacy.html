Privacy policy

rclone-backup is used only by its owner, to back up the owner's own files to the owner's own Google Drive.

  • It only accesses files in Google Drive that it has created itself (the drive.file scope).
  • Files are encrypted before upload. No data is sent to, stored by or shared with any third party.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking are used.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
